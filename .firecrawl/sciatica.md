## What is Sciatica?

The sciatic nerve is the longest nerve in the body, and it lies in your lower back around the lumbar spine. Sciatica occurs because something has irritated or put pressure on that nerve, which makes pain radiate down the legs. When this becomes a chronic condition, it's known as sciatica. Spinal misalignments, excess weight, and disc disorders can all act as the trigger for sciatica.

## Common Causes of Sciatica

Understanding the potential causes of sciatica can help relieve pain for those dealing with the condition. It can also be helpful for people looking to prevent sciatica to understand what puts them at greater risk of developing it.

Sciatica is often a result of one of the following common causes that can benefit from conservative treatment:

Disc Problems

Herniated discs, bulging discs, or slipped discs can contribute to pressure on the sciatic nerve, causing severe pain to run down affected areas.

Degenerative Disk Disease

As discs in the spine wear down, they become thinner and less flexible. This can irritate the nerves around them and cause sciatic nerve pain when sciatic nerve compression occurs.

Spinal Stenosis

When the spaces in your spine narrow, they can squeeze the nerves in the spinal canal. This can cause pain symptoms to occur and lead to sciatica.

Injuries

Injuries from sports or other physical traumas can lead to sciatica symptoms appearing.

Pregnancy

Pressure on the body due to extra weight from pregnancy can put pressure on the nerve roots that make the sciatic nerve or on the sciatic nerve itself.

Osteoarthritis

As the cartilage in your spine wears down, it can cause pain to radiate out and put pressure on nearby nerves.

Spondylolisthesis

Occurs when one vertebra slips over another. This can pinch nerves and lead to sciatic pain.

Tumors

Tumors on the spine can press on the sciatic nerve or its roots. However, this occurrence is rare.

Infections

Infections in or around the spine can have an impact on the sciatic nerve.

## What Symptoms Does Sciatica Cause?

Sciatica symptoms can vary in type and severity. Some of the symptoms that benefit from an evaluation at St. Louis Pain Center include:

- Low back pain

- Tingling down legs

- Leg numbness

## Frequently Asked Questions Regarding Sciatica

Can a pain management provider fix sciatica?

Yes, a pain management clinic can help fix sciatica. Sciatica is often caused by pressure on the sciatic nerve due to excess weight or other sources of pressure on the sciatic nerve. Pain management clinics can help fix the condition by addressing the root causes of the pain.

How to alleviate sciatic pain?

In the short term, things like alternating heat and cold, stretches, and pain medication can help alleviate the symptoms of sciatica. For lasting relief, though, you need to address whatever is putting pressure on or irritating the sciatic nerve.

Can sciatica be cured permanently?

Yes, with the right therapies and treatments, sciatica can be permanently cured. Pain management services are the best way to permanently get rid of sciatica, as it can help remove the root cause of the condition.

What's the worst thing you can do for sciatica?

High-impact exercises along with twisting and bending motions are the most damaging activities that can be performed while suffering from sciatica.
