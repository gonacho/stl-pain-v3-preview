## What are Semaglutide and Tirzepatide?

Semaglutide is a prescription weight loss medication that started life as a way to treat and manage type 2 diabetes. Eventually, it was found that semaglutide works as a medication for long-term weight loss and weight management. Semaglutide is typically used as a weekly injection that works to suppress appetite in patients who use it. By eating less, it becomes easier to lose weight.

Tirzepatide is an FDA-approved drug that helps treat obesity and aids in weight management. It is a GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide) receptor and a GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) receptor agonist. This means that when injected, it activates certain receptors and proteins in the body to reduce food cravings and keep people feeling full for longer.

## FAQs Related To Medical Weight Loss

Is semaglutide worth it for weight loss?

Semaglutide has proven effective as a healthy weight loss medication for those determined to reduce calories and reduce their weight.

What exactly does semaglutide do to your body?

Semaglutide works by making your body feel more full after eating. In doing so, you eat less food. When this is combined with exercise and proper guidance, the treatments can reduce your weight and improve your overall health.

What is the average weekly weight loss on Tirzepatide?

In clinical trials like the SURMOUNT-5 study, patients taking tirzepatide achieved an average total weight loss of up to 20.2% of their body weight. Those using semaglutide reached an average reduction of 13.7%. These results reflect the medications’ strong potential when used with proper support.

What should you avoid while taking Tirzepatide?

Foods that are high in sugar and high in fat should be avoided while taking Tirzepatide, as they can affect your weight loss. Alcohol should also be avoided while taking Tirzepatide due to its negative interaction with the drug.
