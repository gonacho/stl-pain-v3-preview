## What are PRP Injections?

PRP injections assist in tissue regeneration and pain reduction in the body. The blood plasma is filtered out of a sample of the patient's blood, which is then injected back into the target area. The typical injection site for these treatments is the joints, where they help bind the destructive enzymes that cause cartilage degradation and cartilage breakdown, which leads to arthritis. These injections require minimal downtime, and many patients find that the injections not only get to the root cause of their condition but are highly effective against conditions like osteoarthritis.

PRP is an excellent non-invasive option for people looking for alternatives to medications. With proper care and the help of these restorative treatments, it's possible for people to live a normal life without the pain of arthritis and other joint conditions.

## FAQs Regarding PRP Therapy

Is PRP treatment painful?

Most people feel little to no pain. A numbing cream is applied before the procedure to keep you comfortable. Some may feel slight pressure or a mild tingling sensation, but it’s quick and well tolerated.

How long does a PRP session take?

A session usually lasts about 45 minutes to an hour. This includes drawing your blood, processing it, and applying or injecting the plasma. There’s no long downtime, so you can go back to your day soon after.

What is PRP therapy good for?

PRP therapy is an effective treatment option for chronic conditions that affect the joints, like osteoarthritis, or for healing soft tissue damage.

How painful is PRP?

Because PRP is a minimally invasive procedure, patients will only experience minimal pain. If patients are especially worried about the pain, providers will often offer a numbing agent to help.
