## What is Knee Pain?

Knee pain is a common form of joint pain and entails any sensation of discomfort that occurs in the knee. It can be caused by a chronic condition or by an injury. The most common cause of knee pain is osteoarthritis, a chronic condition that wears down the cartilage that cushions the joints.

## Common Causes of Knee Pain

Understanding the potential causes of knee pain can help you prevent it in the future. For those already experiencing knee problems, it can be helpful to understand the causes in order to identify what may have caused your condition.

Knee pain is often the result of one of a variety of factors:

Ligament Injury

Injuries or tears in the ACL are a common cause of knee pain in athletes.

Fractures

Falls and auto accidents can cause breaks or fractures in the knee. Bones weakened by arthritis are also more likely to fracture due to mundane reasons like a bad step.

Torn Meniscus

Sudden twists to the knee while lifting weights or putting strain on the knees can tear the meniscus.

Knee Bursitis

The fluid-filled sacs, called bursae, that cushion the joints can swell up and cause pain in the affected area.

Osteoarthritis

Osteoarthritis is the most common form of arthritis and is a degenerative disease that causes the cartilage in the knee to deteriorate.

## What Symptoms Does Knee Pain Cause?

Symptoms of knee pain can vary in type and severity. Some of the symptoms that benefit from an evaluation at St. Louis Pain Center include:

- Swelling

- Stiffness

- Redness and warmth to touch

- Weakness or instability

- Popping or crunching sounds when moving

- Inability to fully straighten your knee

## FAQs About Knee Pain

What are three signs of a meniscus tear in the knee?

Popping sensations, swelling or stiffness, and pain when twisting or rotating the knee are three of the most common signs of a meniscus tear. If you are experiencing any of these symptoms, you should seek medical care.

Do knee braces help with knee pain?

Orthotics like knee braces can help support the knee joint and alleviate knee pain.

How long is too long for knee pain?

If your knee pain persists after a week, it is time to seek medical attention. Knee pain that lasts that long is usually an indicator of a larger issue.

What is the average age for knee pain?

While knee pain can occur at any time in life, it is most common to see knee pain occur between 55 and 64.
