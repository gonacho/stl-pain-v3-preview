## FAQs Related To Hyaluronic Acid Injections

Do hyaluronic acid injections really work?

The effectiveness of HA injections depends on the patient and the severity of their condition. In most cases, mild to moderate conditions can be treated with HA injections. More severe conditions tend to be less consistent in the relief they receive.

How long do hyaluronic acid injections last?

HA injections can last in a range of 4-6 months. In some cases, though, they lasted as long as a year. The exact length of time varies depending on the patient and their condition.

Who should not get hyaluronic acid injections?

People with bleeding or blood clotting disorders, certain allergies, or an infection in the joint or skin around the target joint should avoid getting HA injections.
