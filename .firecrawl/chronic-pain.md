## What is Chronic Pain?

Chronic pain is recurring or persistent sensations of pain that occur because of an underlying medical condition. Some people may not even be aware of the condition causing their pain, making it more difficult to treat.

At St. Louis Pain Center, we are dedicated to helping our patients make a full recovery from whatever is ailing them. We provide the best therapeutic treatments for chronic pain and never utilize addictive medications or invasive surgeries.

If you’re suffering from chronic pain, schedule an appointment with our pain management clinic today. Our staff is ready to work with you to alleviate your condition.

## Common Causes of Chronic Pain

Understanding the potential causes of chronic pain can help you prevent it in the future. For those already experiencing chronic pain problems, it can be helpful to understand the causes in order to identify what may have caused your condition.

Chronic pain is often the result of one of a variety of factors:

Arthritis

Arthritis is a common chronic condition that causes pain in the joints.

Injuries

Injuries can cause chronic pain if not properly treated.

Cancer

Cancer pain is a common cause of chronic pain as patients work to manage the condition.

Fibromyalgia

Fibromyalgia is a condition that causes chronic pain in the muscles.

Neuropathic Pain

Nerve damage from conditions like Neuropathy can cause chronic pain as the damaged nerves send signals up through the body.

Sciatica

Sciatica pain can often be chronic due to the underlying cause never getting treated properly. Getting patients care for the cause of the sciatica is the key for lasting healing.

## FAQs Related to Chronic Pain

What qualifies as chronic pain?

Any pain that lasts longer than three months or is the result of a chronic condition is considered chronic pain.

What is the best cure for chronic pain?

While there is no cure for chronic pain, managing the condition through medical treatments and lifestyle changes often allows people to live normal lives with significantly reduced pain.

Does chronic pain ever go away?

Chronic pain never fully goes away, however it can be managed to mitigate the frequency and intensity of the pain.

How do I know if my pain is chronic?

Your pain is considered chronic if it lasts for three months or longer. Beyond three months your pain has now persisted for longer than most injuries or illnesses would need to heal.
