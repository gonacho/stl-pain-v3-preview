## What is Regenerative Therapy?

Regenerative therapy is a non-invasive treatment approach that supports the body’s natural healing abilities. It works by enhancing cellular communication, which helps promote tissue repair, reduce inflammation, and strengthen immune response. This process makes it a promising option for addressing conditions such as neuropathy and chronic joint pain.

At St. Louis Pain Center, we collaborate with you to design a personalized care plan that targets the root cause of your symptoms. Our goal is to help you regain function, restore comfort, and return to the activities you love.
