## What is Back Pain?

Back pain can come from a variety of sources ranging from poor posture to injury. Some people notice the symptoms of their back pain as soon as they occur while others will take more time to notice.

## Common Causes of Back Pain

Understanding the potential causes of back pain can help you prevent it in the future. For those already experiencing back problems, it can be helpful to understand the causes in order to identify what may have caused your condition.

Back pain is often the result of one of a variety of factors:

Poor Posture

Poor posture is often a result of long periods of desk work or from staring down at devices for prolonged periods. In recent years, posture issues have become a leading cause of back pain.

Muscle Strain

Frequent strain or overuse of back muscles increases the chance of causing back pain.

Injuries

Sports injuries and other traumatic events or work can cause severe or chronic back pain.

Pinched Nerves

Pinched nerves occur when the vertebrae in your back compress or you are suffering from a herniated disc. Depending on where in your back this occurs, it can also cause sciatica pain.

Stress

A typical stress response involves tightening the muscles in the neck and shoulders. The tension this causes can lead to back pain.

Obesity

The lack of an active lifestyle can cause long term pain and lower back pain due to the excess strain placed on the spine.

## FAQs About Back Pain

What are the most common causes of back pain?

Poor posture and injuries are the two most common causes of back pain. Poor posture has seen a dramatic increase in how many people experience back pain as a result of it. Hunching over phones, computers, and desks at work has led many people to experience back pain as a result of their flawed posture.

How do I know if my back pain is serious?

If your back pain is reoccurring or has begun to noticeably impact your quality of life, it has become serious, and you need to seek medical care in order to have it treated by a medical professional.

How to tell if back pain is muscular or something else?

A medical provider can make a proper pain diagnosis to determine if your back pain is the result of something in your muscles, spine, or nerves. If you are uncertain about the source of your pain, schedule an appointment with a chiropractor and let them take a look and potentially provide the pain relief you need.

How can I get rid of my back pain?

Rest and alternating heat and cold are good at-home remedies for minor instances of back pain. In more severe cases of back pain, though, you will need the help of a medical provider in order to make a proper recovery.
