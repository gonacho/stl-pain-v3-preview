Neuropathy and Pain Treatment, St. Louis, MO

Neuropathy pain and other forms of chronic pain can be a frustrating condition to live with. Neuropathy causes muscle weakness, burning pain, tingling, and numbness to shoot down the legs. This is the result of nerve damage to the peripheral nerves in the foot. If you're suffering from peripheral neuropathy or chronic pain, you need the help of a provider that has the knowledge in order to help treat your nerve pain.

At St. Louis Pain Center, we help our patients find pain relief from their chronic nerve discomfort. Our treatments can help you to better manage neuropathic pain and even help peripheral nerves heal. Symptoms of Peripheral Neuropathy

Neuropathy can cause a variety of symptoms that can impact your daily life. Some of the symptoms caused by neuropathy include:

· Numbness or tingling in the feet and hands

· Sharp, jabbing, or burning pain

· Extreme sensitivity to touch

· Abnormal pain

· Lack of coordination due to damaged motor nerves

· Debilitating balance problems

· Muscle weakness
