# Design Concept Boards — St. Louis Pain Center

## Axis of Variation
**Mood** — three emotionally distinct executions of "Bold and Modern" for a pain management clinic.

The mood axis was chosen over composition or color-emphasis because:

1. **"Bold and modern" is too broad to default to one execution.** A "bold" pain clinic could command fear/respect (Concept 1), inspire hope/momentum (Concept 2), or earn intellectual trust (Concept 3) — all are valid, all are genuinely different, and Bobbee needs to pick which emotional frequency matches how Dr. Arconati sees his practice.
2. **Composition differences alone are cosmetic.** Asymmetric vs. centered is a layout choice that can be varied within a mood — it doesn't define the identity.
3. **Color-emphasis variation would produce three concepts that feel too similar.** Since the underlying brand is all "navy + teal/accent + white," the color-emphasis differences would be subtle and not give Bobbee a meaningful choice.
4. **taste-skill + emilkowalski both reinforce this:** taste-skill's DESIGN_VARIANCE:8 baseline pushes each concept toward asymmetric editorial layouts anyway — the differentiation needs to come from the emotional register, not the grid structure. Emil Kowalski's "beauty is leverage" principle is most powerful when the emotional message is clear and intentional.

---

## Concept 1: Clinical Command
- Folder: /design-concepts/concept-1-clinical-command/
- Direction: Dark navy dominant (#0A1E3C) with electric teal precision accents (#00B8D4). Asymmetric split-screen hero with diagonal clip-path geometry. Cabinet Grotesk in massive bold weight. Stats as oversized teal numbers. Cold, precise, commanding — like a tier-1 neuroscience center.
- Best for: Patients (and referring physicians) who respond to credentials and clinical authority first. Also strongest for B2B referral traffic. Works especially well if Dr. Arconati wants to position as the most technically advanced pain clinic in St. Louis.

## Concept 2: Recovery Momentum
- Folder: /design-concepts/concept-2-recovery-momentum/
- Direction: Warm navy (#1B3A5C) + warm gold accents (#C8A04A) + teal secondary (#0E7C9E). Split-screen hero with patient/doctor interaction imagery, patient journey steps (1→2→3). Satoshi + Plus Jakarta Sans. Warm, trustworthy, forward-moving — positions pain relief as an achievable journey.
- Best for: Direct patient acquisition, especially for chronic pain patients who have been suffering for months or years and need to feel understood before they'll book. Strongest for the neuropathy and chronic pain keyword targets. Highest emotional conversion potential.

## Concept 3: Expert Editorial
- Folder: /design-concepts/concept-3-expert-editorial/
- Direction: Near-black (#0D0D0D) alternating with warm cream (#F0EEE9). Editorial blue (#2B4EAF) accents. Playfair Display + Satoshi typographic mix (serif + sans editorial tension). Hard-cut section alternation, no gradients, no rounded buttons. Like a premium medical journal.
- Best for: Patients who heavily research their care providers before booking — the type who reads PubMed and wants to see that the clinic understands pain physiology deeply. Strong for Dr. Arconati's authority positioning and the "Authoritative + Educational" tone directive from the client brief. Also the most visually distinctive identity in the St. Louis pain market.

## Recommendation
**Tech Builder recommendation: Concept 1 — Clinical Command.**

Rationale:
- The existing hero video (`hero-bg.mp4`) has a clinical, high-tech procedure room aesthetic that maps naturally onto Concept 1's dark navy + electric teal palette. The video will carry forward without color clash.
- "Bold and modern" + "Authoritative" maps most directly to Concept 1's commanding visual language. Commands immediate authority as required by the client brief.
- taste-skill's DESIGN_VARIANCE:8 baseline (asymmetric, high-contrast) is most fully realized in Concept 1. The angular diagonal clip-path geometry is a distinctive visual signature that differentiates from typical healthcare sites.
- Electric teal (#00B8D4) as the accent is closest to the existing stlpaincenter.com brand colors (existing site uses blue/teal primary), providing visual brand continuity for existing patients during migration.
- Stats-forward hero (500+ Patients, 15 Years Experience, 98% Relief Rate) directly addresses the "Pain control clinic" GBP primary category — puts clinical outcomes front and center.

Concepts 2 and 3 are both excellent alternatives. Concept 2 is the highest-conversion choice if Bobbee wants to optimize for emotional patient acquisition over clinical authority projection. Concept 3 is the most editorially distinctive and would earn the most design recognition, but is the farthest from typical pain clinic expectations and may require patient re-education.

**Bobbee's selection should be documented in `client-brief.md > design_concept_selection` and the winning concept folder copied to `/design-concepts/APPROVED/` before Phase 2 begins.**
