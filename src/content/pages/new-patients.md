## New Patients at St. Louis Pain Center

Welcome to St. Louis Pain Center. We treat patients with chronic pain, neuropathy, orthopedic conditions, and musculoskeletal injuries at our clinic in south St. Louis County. Getting started is straightforward.

## Schedule Your Appointment

**Phone:** Call (314) 846-2100 Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

**Online:** Complete the form below to request your appointment. Our team will confirm your visit within one business day.

Most new patients are scheduled within the same week.

## What to Bring to Your First Visit

- Valid photo identification
- Insurance card (front and back)
- Complete list of current medications and dosages
- Any relevant medical records, imaging, or test results from other providers
- A written description of your symptoms, including when they started and what makes them better or worse

## What to Expect

Your first appointment typically lasts 45 to 60 minutes.

### Step 1: Medical History Review
Your care team reviews your health history, current medications, prior treatments, and imaging. This establishes a baseline.

### Step 2: Physical Examination
A focused physical exam identifies the specific area, type, and severity of your pain. Range of motion testing, neurological screening, and palpation help pinpoint the source.

### Step 3: Treatment Plan Discussion
Based on your exam findings, your care team recommends a treatment plan from our 11 specialized services. This may include [pain control procedures](/pain-control/), [orthopedic treatments](/orthopedic/), [physical therapy](/sports-medicine/), or a combination approach.

You will leave your first visit with a clear understanding of what is causing your pain and the steps we recommend to address it.

## Insurance and Payment

St. Louis Pain Center accepts Medicare and most major commercial insurance plans, including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, and Cigna. [View full insurance details](/insurance/).

Call (314) 846-2100 before your visit to verify coverage for your specific plan and service.
