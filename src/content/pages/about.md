## About St. Louis Pain Center

St. Louis Pain Center is a pain control clinic serving patients across south St. Louis County from our location at 4455 Telegraph Rd #250 in St. Louis, MO. We specialize in treating chronic pain, peripheral neuropathy, orthopedic conditions, and musculoskeletal injuries through non-surgical and minimally invasive approaches.

## Our Approach to Pain Management

Most pain clinics follow one of two paths: medication or referral to surgery. Neither addresses the full picture for most patients.

St. Louis Pain Center takes a third path. We combine 11 specialized services spanning [pain control](/pain-control/), [orthopedic care](/orthopedic/), [medical weight loss](/weight-loss/), and [physical therapy](/sports-medicine/) into coordinated treatment plans built around each patient's diagnosis.

This means your care team can move between nerve blocks, injection therapy, regenerative medicine, and rehabilitation without sending you to multiple facilities. Every treatment happens under one roof.

## Why We Focus on Pain Control

Pain is the most common reason Americans visit a doctor. It is also one of the most poorly treated conditions in healthcare. Too many patients cycle through generic prescriptions, wait months for specialist referrals, and end up no better than when they started.

The Midwest's sedentary work culture, high rates of obesity and diabetes, and harsh winters compound the problem. St. Louis residents face elevated rates of chronic pain and peripheral neuropathy compared to national averages. These conditions require specialized care, not general medicine.
