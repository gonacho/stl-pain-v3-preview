## Reclaiming Your Life From Chronic Pain

Living with pain changes everything. Simple tasks become obstacles. Sleep suffers. Work performance drops. Relationships strain under the weight of something invisible to everyone around you.

St. Louis Pain Center exists because too many patients in the St. Louis metro have been told to just manage their pain with medication alone. That approach fails most people. The Midwest's desk-job culture, high obesity rates, and cold winters create a perfect storm for chronic pain and peripheral neuropathy. These conditions demand more than a prescription pad.

Our clinic takes a different approach. We combine advanced pain control techniques, orthopedic treatments, and rehabilitation services under one roof. Every treatment plan starts with understanding what is actually causing your pain, not just masking the symptoms.

## Pain Control Services in St. Louis, MO

When pain disrupts your daily life, you need targeted intervention. Our pain control services address the root cause of your discomfort through proven, evidence-based techniques.

### Nerve Blocks

Targeted nerve block injections interrupt pain signals at the source. Effective for [back pain](/conditions/back-pain/), [neck pain](/conditions/neck-pain/), and [sciatica](/conditions/sciatica/) that has not responded to conservative care. [Learn about nerve blocks](/pain-control/nerve-blocks/)

### Injection Therapy

Therapeutic injections deliver anti-inflammatory medication directly to damaged tissue. Our injection therapy protocols treat [back pain](/conditions/back-pain/), [shoulder pain](/conditions/shoulder-pain/), and [neck pain](/conditions/neck-pain/) with precision targeting. [Explore injection therapy options](/pain-control/injection-therapy/)

### Kyphoplasty

A minimally invasive procedure that stabilizes spinal compression fractures and restores vertebral height. Kyphoplasty provides rapid relief for patients with fracture-related [back pain](/conditions/back-pain/). [Learn about kyphoplasty](/pain-control/kyphoplasty/)

### Medication Management

Personalized medication protocols for [chronic pain](/conditions/chronic-pain/), [fibromyalgia](/conditions/fibromyalgia/), and [diabetic neuropathy](/conditions/diabetic-neuropathy/). We monitor dosing, adjust combinations, and track outcomes to keep your regimen effective without unnecessary escalation. [Explore medication management](/pain-control/medication-management/)

### Neuropathy Treatment

Peripheral neuropathy affects millions of Americans, and St. Louis residents face elevated risk due to regional diabetes and obesity rates. Our neuropathy treatment program addresses nerve damage through multiple modalities. [Get neuropathy treatment](/pain-control/neuropathy-treatment/)

### Neuromodulation Technique

A drug-free approach that retrains nerve signaling patterns. Neuromodulation treats [neuropathy](/conditions/neuropathy/), [chronic pain](/conditions/chronic-pain/), [fibromyalgia](/conditions/fibromyalgia/), and [vertigo](/conditions/vertigo-vestibular-disorders/) without medication side effects. [Discover neuromodulation](/pain-control/neuromodulation-technique/)

[View all pain control services](/pain-control/)

## Orthopedic Clinic in St. Louis, MO

Joint damage and cartilage loss do not always require surgery. Our orthopedic services use regenerative and restorative techniques to rebuild function and reduce pain.

### Hyaluronic Acid Injections

Gel injections restore joint cushioning for patients with [knee pain](/conditions/knee-pain/), [joint pain](/conditions/joint-pain/), and [arthritis](/conditions/arthritis-osteoarthritis/). A series of weekly injections can provide months of improved mobility. [Learn about hyaluronic acid injections](/orthopedic/hyaluronic-acid-injections/)

### PRP Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma concentrates your body's own healing factors and delivers them directly to damaged tissue. PRP therapy accelerates recovery for [knee pain](/conditions/knee-pain/), [joint pain](/conditions/joint-pain/), and [shoulder pain](/conditions/shoulder-pain/). [Explore PRP therapy](/orthopedic/prp-therapy/)

### Regenerative Therapy

Advanced regenerative medicine harnesses your body's repair mechanisms to treat [joint pain](/conditions/joint-pain/), [arthritis](/conditions/arthritis-osteoarthritis/), and [knee pain](/conditions/knee-pain/) at the cellular level. [Discover regenerative therapy](/orthopedic/regenerative-therapy/)

[View all orthopedic services](/orthopedic/)

## Weight Loss Clinic in St. Louis, MO

Excess weight amplifies joint stress, accelerates cartilage breakdown, and worsens chronic pain conditions. Our physician-supervised **medical weight loss** program uses clinically proven medications including tirzepatide and semaglutide. In the SURMOUNT-5 trial, tirzepatide patients achieved 20.2% body weight reduction compared to 13.7% with semaglutide. Weight loss directly reduces pain in weight-bearing joints and improves outcomes for [arthritis](/conditions/arthritis-osteoarthritis/), [joint pain](/conditions/joint-pain/), and [chronic pain](/conditions/chronic-pain/).

[Start your weight loss journey](/weight-loss/)
