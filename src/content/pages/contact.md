## Contact St. Louis Pain Center

**Address:** 4455 Telegraph Rd #250, St. Louis, MO 63129

**Phone:** (314) 846-2100

**Hours:**
- Monday through Thursday: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Friday through Sunday: Closed

## Get Directions

St. Louis Pain Center is located in south St. Louis County on Telegraph Road, near the intersection with Lemay Ferry Road. We are easily accessible from I-55, I-270, and Highway 30.

**From Oakville/Mehlville:** Head north on Telegraph Rd. Our building is on the right side, Suite 250.

**From Affton/Crestwood:** Take Gravois Rd east to Telegraph Rd, then south approximately 3 miles.

**From Arnold/Fenton:** Take I-55 North to the Telegraph Rd exit. Head north approximately 2 miles.

Free parking is available in the building lot.

## New Patient Information

First-time patients can [schedule an appointment online](/new-patients/) or call (314) 846-2100. Please bring:

- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- List of current medications
- Relevant medical records or imaging (if available)
- Completed new patient forms (available at your appointment)

## Insurance

We accept Medicare and most major commercial insurance plans, including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, and Cigna. For coverage questions or to verify your specific plan, call our office before your visit. [View insurance information](/insurance/).

## Have Questions?

Call **(314) 846-2100** during business hours. Our team responds to phone inquiries Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## Contact FAQs

### What are your office hours?
Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed Friday through Sunday.

### Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment?
Contact our office at (314) 846-2100 to confirm whether your insurance plan requires a referral for your specific service.

### Where is your office located?
4455 Telegraph Rd #250, St. Louis, MO 63129, in south St. Louis County. We are close to I-55 and accessible from Oakville, Mehlville, Affton, and surrounding communities.

### Do you accept walk-in patients?
We recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure availability. Call (314) 846-2100 for same-day appointment options.
