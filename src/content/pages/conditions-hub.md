## Conditions We Treat in St. Louis

St. Louis Pain Center diagnoses and treats a wide range of pain conditions. Every condition page below explains what causes it, how we treat it, and what to expect from care at our clinic.

If you are not sure which condition matches your symptoms, call **(314) 846-2100** to schedule an evaluation. Our care team identifies the source of your pain and recommends the right treatment path.

## Neuropathy and Nerve Conditions

### [Neuropathy (Peripheral Neuropathy)](/conditions/neuropathy/)
Damage to peripheral nerves causes numbness, tingling, burning, and pain in the hands and feet. St. Louis Pain Center specializes in neuropathy diagnosis and treatment through [neuropathy treatment](/pain-control/neuropathy-treatment/) and [neuromodulation](/pain-control/neuromodulation-technique/).

### [Diabetic Neuropathy](/conditions/diabetic-neuropathy/)
Nerve damage caused by chronic high blood sugar. The most common form of neuropathy, affecting up to 50% of people with diabetes.

### [Neuropathy in Feet](/conditions/neuropathy-in-feet/)
Burning, tingling, and numbness in the feet often signals peripheral nerve damage. This high-impact symptom interferes with walking, balance, and daily function.

## Musculoskeletal Pain Conditions

### [Back Pain](/conditions/back-pain/)
The leading cause of disability worldwide. We treat back pain through [nerve blocks](/pain-control/nerve-blocks/), [injection therapy](/pain-control/injection-therapy/), [kyphoplasty](/pain-control/kyphoplasty/), and [physical therapy](/sports-medicine/).

### [Neck Pain](/conditions/neck-pain/)
Cervical spine conditions, muscle strain, and nerve compression all cause neck pain. Treatment options include nerve blocks, injection therapy, and physical therapy.

### [Knee Pain](/conditions/knee-pain/)
Arthritis, meniscus injuries, and cartilage loss are the most common causes. We treat knee pain with [hyaluronic acid injections](/orthopedic/hyaluronic-acid-injections/), [PRP therapy](/orthopedic/prp-therapy/), and [regenerative therapy](/orthopedic/regenerative-therapy/).

### [Joint Pain](/conditions/joint-pain/)
Pain in any joint, from hips to shoulders to hands. Our orthopedic services restore cushioning and promote tissue repair without surgery.

### [Shoulder Pain](/conditions/shoulder-pain/)
Rotator cuff injuries, frozen shoulder, and tendon damage respond well to PRP therapy, injection therapy, and physical therapy.

### [Sciatica](/conditions/sciatica/)
Sciatic nerve compression causes radiating pain from the lower back through the leg. Nerve blocks, kyphoplasty, and physical therapy target the underlying cause.

## Chronic and Systemic Pain Conditions

### [Chronic Pain](/conditions/chronic-pain/)
Pain lasting more than 3 months requires a multimodal treatment strategy. We combine medication management, neuromodulation, physical therapy, and targeted procedures.

### [Arthritis / Osteoarthritis](/conditions/arthritis-osteoarthritis/)
Progressive joint cartilage breakdown causes stiffness, swelling, and pain. Hyaluronic acid injections, regenerative therapy, and medical weight loss slow progression and improve mobility.

### [Fibromyalgia](/conditions/fibromyalgia/)
Widespread pain, fatigue, and cognitive difficulty define fibromyalgia. Neuromodulation, medication management, and physical therapy address multiple symptom domains.

### [Vertigo / Vestibular Disorders](/conditions/vertigo-vestibular-disorders/)
Dizziness, balance problems, and spatial disorientation. Neuromodulation technique and vestibular physical therapy provide drug-free management options.
