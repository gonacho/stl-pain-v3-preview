## PRP Therapy vs. Hyaluronic Acid Injections: Which Is Right for You?

Choosing between **PRP therapy** and **hyaluronic acid injections** depends on your specific condition, the severity of joint damage, and your recovery goals. Both treatments avoid surgery. Both deliver results for knee pain, joint pain, and arthritis. They work through completely different mechanisms.

St. Louis Pain Center offers both options. Your care team evaluates your imaging, symptoms, and medical history before recommending one treatment or a combination of both.

## How PRP Therapy Works

**Platelet-rich plasma therapy** concentrates your own blood platelets and injects them into damaged tissue. Platelets contain growth factors (PDGF, TGF-beta, VEGF) that trigger your body's natural repair process. The concentration is typically 5 to 10 times higher than normal blood.

PRP targets the underlying tissue damage. It stimulates new cell growth in tendons, ligaments, and cartilage. Results develop gradually over 4 to 6 weeks as the tissue regenerates.

### Best Candidates for PRP

- Patients with tendon injuries, ligament sprains, or early-stage cartilage loss
- Active individuals who want tissue repair rather than symptom relief alone
- Patients with [shoulder pain](/conditions/shoulder-pain/) from rotator cuff or labral damage
- Those with [knee pain](/conditions/knee-pain/) from meniscus or ligament injuries

## How Hyaluronic Acid Injections Work

**Hyaluronic acid injections** (also called gel injections or viscosupplementation) restore the natural cushioning fluid in your joints. Healthy joints contain hyaluronic acid that lubricates and absorbs shock. Arthritis and aging break down this fluid, leading to bone-on-bone friction.

A series of 3 to 5 weekly injections rebuilds that cushion. Most patients experience 6 to 12 months of improved mobility and reduced pain per treatment cycle.

### Best Candidates for HA Injections

- Patients with moderate to advanced [arthritis/osteoarthritis](/conditions/arthritis-osteoarthritis/)
- Those with [knee pain](/conditions/knee-pain/) from cartilage wear who want to delay or avoid surgery
- Patients who have not responded well to oral anti-inflammatory medication
- Those with [joint pain](/conditions/joint-pain/) in weight-bearing joints

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Factor | PRP Therapy | Hyaluronic Acid Injections |
|--------|------------|---------------------------|
| **Mechanism** | Stimulates tissue regeneration | Restores joint cushioning |
| **Source** | Your own blood (autologous) | Synthetic or animal-derived HA |
| **Sessions** | Typically 1-3 injections | 3-5 weekly injections per cycle |
| **Onset** | Gradual (4-6 weeks) | Faster (1-2 weeks) |
| **Duration** | 6-12+ months | 6-12 months per cycle |
| **Best for** | Tissue damage, tendon/ligament | Cartilage loss, bone-on-bone |
| **Insurance** | Often not covered | Often covered by Medicare |

## Can You Combine Both Treatments?

Yes. Some patients benefit from both approaches used in sequence. HA injections provide immediate cushioning relief while PRP works on longer-term tissue repair. Your care team at St. Louis Pain Center evaluates whether a combined protocol makes sense for your specific joint condition.

## Which Treatment Should You Choose?

The right choice depends on your diagnosis. Patients with significant cartilage loss or bone-on-bone arthritis typically respond better to hyaluronic acid injections. Patients with soft tissue injuries, early-stage arthritis, or tendon damage often see stronger results from PRP therapy.

Neither treatment requires surgery. Neither requires extended downtime. Both are performed in-office at our St. Louis clinic.

## PRP vs. HA Injections FAQs

### Is PRP therapy more effective than hyaluronic acid injections?
They treat different problems. PRP excels at tissue regeneration for tendon and ligament injuries. HA injections excel at restoring joint cushioning for arthritis. Your care team recommends based on your specific diagnosis.

### Does insurance cover PRP or hyaluronic acid injections?
Hyaluronic acid injections are frequently covered by Medicare and commercial insurance plans. PRP therapy coverage varies by insurer. Contact our office at (314) 846-2100 to verify your benefits.

### How long do results last for each treatment?
Both typically provide 6 to 12 months of relief. HA injection cycles can be repeated. PRP results may be longer-lasting when the underlying tissue heals.

### Can I get both treatments at St. Louis Pain Center?
Yes. We offer both PRP therapy and hyaluronic acid injections and frequently combine them in treatment plans for patients with complex joint conditions.
